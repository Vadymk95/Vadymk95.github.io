What I do

Hands-on lead for a team of three across twelve production repositories: public routes on Next.js, internal tools on React and Vue, Java services behind them. I own the frontend quality gates, review every change that ships, and write the standards the team works to.

Most implementation on my side now runs through an AI-assisted setup I built: agents write against a repository's own rules, hard gates stop what is wrong (lint, type-check, tests, bundle size, CI), and I review what is left. The framework is not the constraint: the same workflow and gates carry over to any frontend codebase. Delivery is faster. The review bar is not lower.
